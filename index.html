About this provisional map & bubble sizes

Bubble view includes each distinct node on the selected achievement's explicit path, excluding the achievement itself. It does not invent missing links, propagate impact through parent/child categories, or treat shared nodes as multiple observations.

Choose Performance, Reliability, Scalability, Security, or their equal-weight mean. Circle area is proportional to the chosen 1–10 score: radius = 14 × √score in diagram units (14–44.27). The same scale is used for all nodes. Missing or out-of-range scores use a hollow, unscaled marker. The central glass disc is decorative and is not a scored circle.

These ordinal, provisional ratings are not measurements: twice the displayed area does not establish twice the real-world impact. Color and ring placement are decorative, not extra scores. Scores belong to nodes, not to achievement-specific effects. Diagram fitting changes the screen scale, not within-diagram area ratios.
